# Story — webrivo-short (60s, problem-first)

One spine: the best shop on the street is invisible on a phone → the sale goes
elsewhere → WebRivo gives it a page that gets found, takes bookings and takes
payments, for a monthly fee with no setup cost.

## The problem with the previous cut

② said the shop has no website; ④ listed symptoms of a *bad* website (slow page,
wrong pin, unanswered form). Those can't both be true. Nothing on screen
explained what WebRivo actually sells.

## What is cut, and why

- The old ④ symptoms of a *bad* site (slow page / wrong pin / no reply) — they
  contradicted ②'s "no website". Replaced by OFF MAP / NO PROOF / NO SLOTS, the
  costs of having no page at all, before the same "someone else" turn.
- The ⑤ docket as the only explanation of the product — kept as a short process
  beat (④) and followed by the actual product: a real landing page, booking,
  payment, and the owner's dashboard.
